Calf First. Tradition Always.

Nestled near the holy hills of Barsana, Radha Farms is a sanctuary where indigenous Indian cows graze on mineral-rich green fodder, neem, and tulsi—never factory-fed or tied in cramped sheds.

We strictly practice Ahimsa milking: calves always drink their full mother’s share first. Only the remaining surplus milk is chilled to 4°C and bottled in sanitized glass within 3 hours.

The 4-Step Vedic Bilona Method

Unlike commercial cream-separated butter, our ghee follows the authentic Ayurvedic path.

01
🌱

Ahimsa Milking

Morning milking begins at 4:30 AM after calves drink freely from their mothers.

02
🏺

Matka Curding

Slow-boiled A2 milk is cultured overnight in porous earthen clay pots with live starters.

03
🧈

Bilona Churning

Whole curd is churned back-and-forth with a wooden mathani during early Brahma Muhurta.

04
🔥

Slow Simmering

Pure white makhan is simmered over gentle flame into golden danedar Bilona ghee.
